# Badge of Honor

> Module type: `badge_of_honor` · Category: Content · **Pro**

Animated Pro membership badge with rotating text and gradient effects.

A small, fun status indicator that lights up when you're signed into a Pro account. Useful to verify your Pro status at a glance, or to add a bit of polish to a kiosk dashboard.

## Configuration

- **Size** — small / medium / large
- **Display text** — rotating text (defaults to your username + "Pro")
- **Gradient colors** — primary and accent colors
- **Animation speed**
- **Show username** — toggle

## Notes

- The badge only animates when you're signed in to UltraCard.io and the **Pro Cloud** integration is healthy.
- It's purely cosmetic — there are no actions or entity hooks.
